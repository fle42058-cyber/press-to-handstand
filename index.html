How it works. 8 physical attributes (mobility, strength, balance…) drive 10 progression drills from wall slides to the full press. A genetic algorithm evolves the weights of a neural-network "coach" that picks, each week, how to focus your training so the built-in progression simulator reaches the press fastest with the fewest overreach injuries. Your self-assessment personalises it. The greedy baseline guarantees a sensible curriculum while the network keeps refining.
Engine: Rust (std-only). UI: TypeScript. Server: /api/evolution · /api/meta · /api/recommend
